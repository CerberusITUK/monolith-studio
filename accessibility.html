Our commitment to making our website usable for everyone.

Last updated: August 2026

Our Commitment

Monolith Studio is committed to ensuring digital accessibility for all users, including those with disabilities. We aim to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at level AA where possible.

Measures We Take

To improve accessibility, we:

— Use semantic HTML and proper heading structures throughout the site.

— Provide text alternatives and aria labels for interactive elements.

— Ensure sufficient colour contrast between text and background.

— Support keyboard navigation for all interactive components.

— Respect prefers-reduced-motion to minimise animations for users who prefer reduced motion.

— Design responsive layouts that adapt to different screen sizes and devices.

Known Limitations

Our website features a 3D particle animation powered by Three.js. While we provide fallbacks and reduced-motion support, some interactive 3D content may not be fully accessible to all users. If you experience difficulty, please contact us.

Compatibility

The website is designed to work with modern browsers including Chrome, Firefox, Safari, and Edge on desktop and mobile devices.

Feedback

If you encounter an accessibility issue or have suggestions for improvement, please email us at studio@monolith.creative. We take all feedback seriously and will make reasonable efforts to address issues promptly.
