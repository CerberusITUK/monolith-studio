What cookies we use and why.

Last updated: August 2026

What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help the site remember you and provide a better experience.

Cookies We Use

Our website uses the following types of cookies:

— Essential cookies: Required for the website to function correctly. These cannot be disabled.

— Analytics cookies: Help us understand how visitors interact with our site so we can improve it. These are anonymous and aggregate.

— Preference cookies: Remember your settings and preferences for a more personalised experience.

Third-Party Cookies

Some third-party services we use (e.g. Google Fonts, analytics providers) may set their own cookies. These are governed by the respective providers' privacy policies.

Managing Cookies

You can control and delete cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to refuse cookies or alert you when cookies are being sent. See your browser's help documentation for details.

Note that disabling cookies may affect the functionality of our website.
