Twelve people across London and Tokyo, obsessed with the space between disciplines. We treat every project like a mission — researched, rehearsed, and launched with intent.

What we believe

Craft is a
team sport.

Monolith was founded on a simple idea: the best work happens when strategy, design, film, and code live under one roof. No agencies handing off down the line. No lost threads. Just one crew, one vision, from first conversation to final frame.

We're independent, deliberately small, and picky about what we take on. Six to eight projects a year, maximum. That's how we keep the quality high and the work honest.
